How it works

From enquiry to registration

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Call or visit with your parent documents. We understand your requirement and explain the right document.

  2. 2

    Verification

    Parent deeds, patta, EC and ID proofs are checked, and guideline value and stamp duty are calculated.

  3. 3

    Drafting

    The document is drafted in Tamil or English and read over with you before finalising.

  4. 4

    Registration

    Online submission, token booking and support on the day of registration at the Sub-Registrar Office.

Documents usually required

  • Parent document / previous sale deed (original)
  • Patta, chitta and adangal copies
  • Latest Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Aadhaar card and PAN card of all parties
  • Passport-size photographs of all parties
  • Property tax / EB receipt (for buildings)
  • Two witnesses with ID proof on registration day

Exact requirements depend on the type of document — please call before visiting.
